# The Relief Circuit asset bible

## Current accepted boundary

The Relief Circuit is Program 005 and the canonical Book III Common Ship reference. Its production presentation is no longer a prepared asset pack waiting for Gate 6. The accepted runtime binds the complete nine-operation campaign, six founding people, vessel-management representation, connected Lamp District operation, exact portable-run custody, and the following project-owned vector set:

- multispecies vessel environment;
- Ilya Venn portrait;
- Nima Quell portrait;
- Orun Sable portrait;
- Tessara One portrait;
- Arden Pell portrait;
- Cinder Continuing portrait;
- seven-system vessel cross-section;
- twenty-one-symbol operational atlas.

The historical provenance manifest remains immutable evidence of when the asset slice was prepared and what it did not yet claim. The program-level rollup records the current accepted boundary without rewriting that history.

## Design law

The ship is one moving polity and cannot be rendered as a continuous human-native office with exotic occupants placed inside it.

The visual system must preserve:

- incompatible environmental media;
- materially different gravity, scale, occupied volume, passage, locomotion, manipulation, and sensorium;
- direct and mediated interfaces;
- temporal structure and life-fraction cost;
- distributed and forked continuity claims;
- the distinction between embodiment profiles and occupations;
- the difference between a ship system, a Common Watch test, and an engine-owned state track;
- neutral presentation for imported Common Ships.

No founding person may be reduced to a reskinned human silhouette when the authored embodiment claims otherwise.

## Founding cast

**Ilya Venn** remains the rapid-response pilot whose direct helm and manual abort authority depend on host-normal physical assumptions that must remain visible as assumptions.

**Nima Quell** remains inside aquatic pressure continuity. Dry-space participation is mediated access rather than effortless conversion into a dry humanoid.

**Orun Sable** occupies a genuinely massive high-gravity body and retains load-bearing structural contact. The composition must not shrink Orun into a large costume.

**Tessara One** is simultaneously represented through carrier, microbial cloud, chemical vessels, and topological signal. No one representation is declared the original person.

**Arden Pell** lives a nine-year cycle. Compressed calendars and life-fraction accounting remain visible around the body and the operational decision.

**Cinder Continuing** appears through several divergent machine embodiments connected by a contested continuity core. Divergence remains political and personal rather than a decorative robot variant.

The marks summarize records. They do not create a universal visual ontology, replace authored labels, or become engine inputs.

## Responsive and access law

Desktop may show the full environment, cross-section, and atlas together. Mobile may crop environments and provide bounded scroll or zoom for structural maps, but it may not drop system relations or labels.

Forced-colors mode may remove decorative vectors. Reduced motion may suppress entry and state animation. Sound may be disabled. Every operation remains readable, operable, and semantically complete through text, controls, and engine receipts.

## Custody

All assets are local Vite-owned files with project provenance. Remote references, embedded raster payloads, script, event handlers, and executable SVG content are prohibited. Asset presence, byte identity, view boxes, vector complexity, descriptions, and program ownership enter the deterministic release inventory.

The assets do not change cartridge identity, run integrity, Common Watch feasibility, state effects, connected-operation law, or trust classification.
