# The 1 AM Player Runtime Gate

The 1 AM player is the cold, tired player who will not read the authoring packet or infer missing continuity. The product must make its own model legible through play.

World must retain machine-readable evidence that a player can answer:

1. Who am I?
2. What am I doing?
3. Why does it matter?
4. What changed because I acted?
5. What can I do next?

The gate is broader than control onboarding. It requires:

- identity, goal, stakes, and first action within 30 seconds;
- a meaningful success within 90 seconds;
- every authored objective to produce its actual interaction, not only a combat count;
- critical reveals during play, before the result screen;
- route choices to alter knowledge, interactions, NPC behavior, route, or world state during play;
- visible persistent world and relationship changes;
- a genuinely playable successor scene;
- recovery to return control within five seconds without discarding completed objectives or replaying exposition.

A deterministic action candidate can pass all action-law gates and still fail this experience gate. `campaignEffect: null` is correct for provisional World execution, but a standalone episode must either complete exact Arc replay and consume the accepted consequence or clearly remain classified as an action-receiver prototype.
