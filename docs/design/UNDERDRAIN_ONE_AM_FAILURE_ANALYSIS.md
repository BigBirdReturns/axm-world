# Underdrain one-a.m. failure analysis

Status: post-v1 product correction.

## Executive finding

The Underdrain player did not fail to understand a coherent authored episode. The delivered product was not a coherent authored episode.

It was a deterministic action receiver wrapped in authored story data. The first repair improved control onboarding while making the causal disconnect worse: the primary `Play` action bypassed the premise, and the simplified objective copy replaced plumbing actions with enemy counts.

The resulting package could prove that a player could move, attack, dodge, survive, complete waves, and receive deterministic success/partial/failure classification. It could not prove that the player knew:

```text
who they were
why the fight existed
what authored commitment they made
what the action changed in Bellwether
what concrete thing they could play next
```

The player report is therefore valid product evidence, not operator misuse.

## Historical acceptance law that should have controlled the build

The First Charter / Program 001 cold-run work had already established the correct product boundary:

```text
fresh profile
  -> one inhabited entry
  -> authored commitment
  -> exact plan
  -> deterministic resolution
  -> accepted consequence
  -> changed place and people
  -> durable receipt
  -> exact reload
  -> concrete next action
```

The earlier zero-explanation review required a cold operator to independently describe the player role, immediate conflict, authored choice, consequence, and next action. A successful browser script was never intended to replace that evidence.

The current `program-of-record.test.ts` later narrowed to a different responsibility: exact program identity, authored-Arc digest, schema support, runtime surfaces, and registry custody. That identity gate remained green while the cold-player product law fell out of the permanent test estate.

## What the Underdrain tests actually certified

### V2 receiver prototype

The package expressly tested and accepted these conditions:

- one click from title to combat;
- premise and authored-method choice remain optional;
- goal copy becomes a simple enemy count;
- competent/cautious/button-masher/idle scripted personas reach deterministic terminal results;
- mobile controls are large and operable;
- first loss is rescued or checkpointed;
- no console or network failures;
- authoring JSON contains characters, state, outcomes, and a next-episode label;
- World emits a provisional candidate with `campaignEffect: null`.

Those tests proved an approachable receiver. They also certified the exact disconnect the player later reported.

### Scripted authored-episode repair

The later local authored-episode package was structurally stronger, but its browser supergate still:

- injected authored state directly through internal functions;
- applied a reference success outcome rather than obtaining an accepted result from ordinary play;
- supplied the expected comprehension identities itself;
- selected a continuation seed that was not a complete next playable episode;
- retained `campaignEffect: null` while presenting local world changes.

That package is diagnostic evidence, not a blind-player acceptance receipt.

## Attribution

### Player or ordinary operator error

None.

The following are expected one-a.m. behavior:

- pressing the primary `Play` button;
- skipping optional premise material;
- failing to remember controls after one prompt;
- dying once;
- pausing or reloading;
- declining to continue when the first consequence has no legible meaning.

A qualifying product must survive those behaviors.

### Candidate-author / implementation operator error

1. **Built from the receiver outward.** The deterministic action spec and browser loop became the definition of completion instead of one component inside an authored episode.
2. **Ignored the historical cold-run acceptance.** The Program 001 blind-player work was not re-read before declaring the demo complete.
3. **Overcorrected onboarding.** Making premise and choice optional solved exposition friction by disconnecting play from authored cause.
4. **Converted authored verbs into kill counters.** `Flush the Spore Valves`, `Hold the Pump Line`, and `Break the Crown Matron` all compiled to waves whose only completion law was `targetDefeats`.
5. **Used privileged personas as player evidence.** Scripted agents knew controls, game state, completion predicates, and expected results.
6. **Audited data instead of staged experience.** The narrative audit proved that JSON packets contained a rail and consequences, not that those causes appeared to the player during play.
7. **Claimed continuity from provisional evidence.** A World candidate with `campaignEffect: null` was described as an evolving world before exact Arc acceptance and persistent campaign mutation existed.
8. **Supplied the answers in the test.** The authored-episode supergate injected the route and success result, then asserted the expected role/conflict/consequence identities.
9. **Misclassified the product.** The correct label was `Underdrain action receiver prototype`, not `playable pilot episode`.

### Project-level debt

1. **Program identity and player comprehension were conflated.** The permanent Program-of-Record test proved digest, schema, and surfaces, while the cold-player law remained in historical PR artifacts.
2. **No reusable one-a.m. receipt existed.** There was no machine-readable way to refuse an author, bot, or assisted operator as the blind-player observer.
3. **No Arc-owned authored-experience contract existed.** Narrative law, action law, outcome facts, next beats, and checkpoint identity were not bound into one cartridge-owned episode definition.
4. **Action objective semantics were under-specified.** The action profile owned labels and enemy kits but no governed player verb, target, or completion kind. A `repair` label could therefore resolve as enemy defeats.
5. **World lacked a cartridge-neutral authored-session host.** The proven Program 001 hall → commitment → plan → resolution → receipt chain was implemented as one product path, not generalized as a reusable post-v1 receiver.
6. **The continuous authority seam stopped too early.** Accepted action results could return into narrative fact authority, but there was no player-facing chain from situation through changed place to the next playable beat.
7. **Standalone acceptance remained provisional.** Without co-located exact Arc replay or another accepted-result lane, a standalone World surface could not honestly commit campaign change.
8. **No implemented-next-beat gate existed.** Naming `Parley at the Root Gate` was sufficient even when no player could enter it.

## Closures now implemented

### World: one-a.m. authored-experience gate

PR #208 introduces:

```text
rodoh-one-am-player-receipt/1
rodoh-one-am-player-evaluation/1
ONE_AM_PLAYER_STANDARD.md
```

The evaluator refuses:

- candidate-author, source-inspecting, walkthrough-assisted, or otherwise non-independent observers;
- first authored decision after 60 seconds;
- first accepted consequence after three minutes;
- primary action that bypasses the authored situation;
- control tutorial in place of an authored commitment;
- choice remembered only as metadata or result copy;
- objective label / player verb mismatch;
- reveal only before or after play;
- absent character methods;
- provisional result presented as campaign consequence;
- absent visible world delta, durable record, exact resume, or next playable action;
- desktop/mobile causal divergence;
- required network dependency;
- missing or mismatched answers to role, conflict, choice, consequence, and next action;
- player abandonment or lack of voluntary continuation.

The tests contain two distinct Underdrain refusals:

1. the V2 action-receiver prototype;
2. the scripted authored-episode self-audit that injected state and supplied its own answers.

### Arc: authored-experience and semantic-objective law

PR #184 introduces:

```text
axm-authored-experience/1
axm.authored-experience@1
```

The contract binds entry, role, ordinary stake, commitments, runtime-visible choice signals, challenge objectives, semantic verbs, targets, completion predicates, in-play reveals, accepted outcome facts, obligations, implemented next experiences, and checkpoint custody.

The canonical Arc validator now refuses:

- missing or invented objective bindings;
- `repair`, `operate`, or `reroute` completed by an enemy-defeat counter;
- `negotiate` without an authored choice;
- commitments with no runtime-visible difference;
- duplicate checkpoint identities;
- unknown or absent nonterminal next experiences;
- duplicate facts, obligations, or story payments;
- an old engine floor.

### Underdrain donor reconciliation

The authored-experience authority has been stacked into the existing Underdrain Arc donor. The cartridge can no longer be advanced honestly without choosing one of two paths:

```text
A. rename the current waves to truthful combat verbs
   and retain the action-receiver-prototype classification;

B. extend deterministic action law and the World/Unity mirror
   with interact, hold, and authored-choice completion,
   then implement the actual plumbing episode.
```

The intended pilot requires path B.

## Remaining implementation gaps

### 1. Deterministic semantic action objectives

The current 30 Hz action state advances a wave only after every pressure enemy is defeated. It must gain optional, content-addressed objective law for:

```text
interact_count
hold_ticks
authored_choice (outside or between action phases)
```

Backward compatibility requirement: cartridges without semantic objective authoring must retain their exact existing action-spec bytes and behavior.

For semantic objectives:

- enemies may provide authored pressure;
- defeating enemies may reduce pressure;
- only the declared interaction, hold, or choice completes the objective;
- the accepted action receipt records semantic progress and completion;
- Arc and the C# mirror must reach the same terminal state.

### 2. Cartridge-neutral authored ExperienceHost

World must consume `axm-authored-experience/1` and stage:

```text
entry
commitment
runtime signals
action handoff
in-play reveals
accepted consequence
changed place
durable record
next experience
```

The host must preserve exact checkpoint and reload behavior and expose only one advancing action per directed beat.

### 3. Accepted-result lane for standalone play

A local browser episode needs an honest acceptance path. Options include:

- a dependency-free Arc replay module bundled into the standalone product;
- a local companion that verifies the trace and returns `axm-action-receipt/1`;
- explicit prototype classification with no campaign mutation.

The product may not display a committed Bellwether world delta while its only authority object says `campaignEffect: null`.

### 4. Underdrain pilot replacement

Required first-session chain:

```text
Mrs. Kett service call
  -> diagnose the living drain
  -> municipal draft and authored method choice
  -> Pump Seven
       diagnose / reroute spore valves under pressure
       operate the purge wheel while the nursery cause becomes visible
       restore the Crown sluice and choose town, nursery, or balanced flow
  -> exact Arc replay
  -> accepted world consequence
  -> changed Bellwether surface
  -> playable Parley at the Root Gate
```

The three entry methods must change runtime information, actor presence, routes, presentation, or affordances without secretly altering deterministic difficulty.

### 5. Blind-player matrix

On one exact candidate identity:

- desktop and mobile cold runs;
- no source, walkthrough, or assistance;
- wrong turn;
- missed prompt;
- first loss;
- retry;
- reload during an incomplete beat;
- reload after consequence;
- long pause;
- role/conflict/choice/consequence/next-action comprehension;
- voluntary continuation;
- second materially different cartridge through the same host.

## Closure condition

This correction is complete when:

1. Underdrain's objective labels match deterministic verbs and predicates.
2. The important fungal-nursery reveal occurs during play.
3. An exact Arc receipt commits visible Bellwether state.
4. Reload restores that state and its record.
5. Root Gate is enterable and playable.
6. A cold, independent player can state who they are, what problem they faced, what they chose, what changed, and what they can do next without explanation.
7. The same receiver passes a second materially different cartridge.

Until then, Underdrain remains an action receiver prototype with valuable architecture, not a completed authored pilot.
