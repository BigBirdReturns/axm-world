# The one-a.m. player standard

Status: post-v1 product law for authored player experiences.

## Controlling environment

The one-a.m. player opens the game tired, alone, without source access, without a designer beside them, and without patience for an architecture demonstration. They skim, press the most obvious control, miss prompts, make an ordinary mistake, pause, reload, and decide quickly whether the experience deserves another minute.

That behavior is not misuse. It is the controlling product environment.

A qualifying first session therefore needs:

- one directed authored chain;
- legible role, place, ordinary stake, and commitment;
- action whose mechanics express the authored verb;
- in-play discovery;
- accepted consequence and persistent change;
- exact resume and concrete successor;
- candidate-owned production representation;
- an unobstructed player-space layout;
- independent comprehension evidence.

A scripted browser can prove reachability, determinism, persistence, accessibility, authority boundaries, production custody, and protected-world geometry. It cannot prove that a cold player understood why they acted or what their action changed.

## Five comprehension questions

After the first authored consequence, a player who received no explanation must be able to answer in their own words:

1. **Who am I here?**
2. **What immediate problem am I trying to solve?**
3. **What did I choose?**
4. **What changed because of it?**
5. **What do I do next?**

The receipt records normalized expected and independently observed identifiers. An author cannot copy expected answers into observation fields and call that comprehension evidence.

## Directed first-session chain

```text
inhabited situation
  -> legible role and ordinary stake
  -> one authored commitment
  -> action whose verbs express that commitment
  -> discovery or pressure revealed during play
  -> accepted consequence
  -> visible persistent world change
  -> durable record
  -> one concrete next playable action
```

Every causal role must have production representation on the player surface where it is encountered. A role declaration, debug renderer, accessible description, or screenshot file is not by itself production representation.

## Hard acceptance rules

### Cold entry

- Fresh storage or a new profile.
- No source code, authoring packet, fixture, walkthrough, or verbal explanation.
- The primary action enters the authored situation, not a generic mechanic.
- Cartridge identity, lead actor, place, and ordinary problem appear on the cold surface.
- Technical authority terms may remain inspectable but are not required primary-path comprehension.
- Tutorial content never occupies space needed by the current decision.

### First meaningful action

- One obvious advancing action per beat.
- No quick-resolve, shell, map, debug, or advanced action competes with authored advance.
- The first authored decision is reachable within sixty seconds on the reference device.
- A control tutorial does not count as the authored decision.

### Story and action coupling

- Objective copy names the verb the player actually performs.
- Repair, operate, reroute, and negotiate cannot secretly resolve through unrelated defeat counts.
- The authored commitment changes information, relationship, route, affordance, presentation, or consequence.
- At least one important fact is learned during play.
- Characters act through their authored methods during the playable sequence.

### Role plan and production coverage

A first-party pilot requires both:

```text
rodoh-representation-plan/1
  what must be represented

rodoh-representation-production/1
  which roles have exact production sources
```

The role plan must bind:

- one cartridge namespace and identity role;
- all required people and portrait/body equivalents;
- idle, active, and complete states for each mechanism;
- persistent-state marks;
- cold entry, commitment, action, consequence, successor, and record surfaces;
- desktop, portrait mobile, short-landscape mobile, and nonvisual equivalents;
- neutral fallback disabled for a first-party candidate.

The production receipt must bind:

- every production role ID;
- exact local source paths;
- media type;
- SHA-256;
- dimensions where applicable;
- explicit `prototype`, `mixed`, or `complete` status.

Release representation fails when:

- production coverage is absent;
- status is `prototype` or `mixed`;
- any declared role lacks production custody;
- a production source has no exact path or digest;
- multiple semantic IDs inflate one prototype renderer into a fictitious asset pack;
- another cartridge's art or neutral fallback substitutes for candidate identity.

An asset count must distinguish:

```text
declared roles
production roles
prototype roles
production sources
```

A role ID is not an independently authored file. A source file is not automatically production art. Accessible text is not a waiver of visual production for a visually staged candidate.

### Protected player space

The rendered world is protected space. Required UI may not obscure it merely because controls remain inside a larger container.

The objective and touch controls must live outside the stage/canvas layout region, or an equivalent native layout must prove zero obstruction.

Reference browser acceptance measures actual rectangles and requires:

- zero intersection between canvas and objective deck;
- zero intersection between canvas and touch cluster;
- zero intersection between canvas and every touch target;
- zero intersection between stage and command deck;
- at least 44 x 44 CSS-pixel touch targets;
- contained label ink;
- no pairwise control overlap;
- sufficient unobstructed rendered-world size.

Both portrait and short-landscape viewports are controlling. “Controls remain inside the stage” is not an acceptable criterion because it can certify the exact obstruction being prevented.

### Consequence and continuity

- Owning authority accepts the result before campaign mutation.
- The accepted result produces a visible world delta, not only receipt prose.
- Changed place, relationship, obligation, resource, or institution remains inspectable.
- Production representation changes according to accepted state rather than only swapping text.
- Durable record survives reload under the same World, Arc, authoring, role-plan, and production identities.
- The next obligation is a concrete playable action.
- The successor is inhabited and represented, not a button pointing to a blank surface.
- Resume restores the exact incomplete beat or completed consequence without fallback.

### Recovery

- Missing a prompt cannot hard-lock the experience.
- The first ordinary loss cannot erase authored context.
- Retry preserves completed beats and states what remains.
- The experience waits for the player and does not punish absence.

### Device dignity

- Desktop and mobile preserve decisions, causal order, consequence, and identity.
- Portrait and short-landscape mobile both qualify.
- Mobile may crop or restage art but cannot remove required identity, mechanism, consequence, or successor representation.
- Reduced motion collapses time, not information.
- Forced colors and high contrast preserve semantic boundaries.
- Muted play loses no required information.

## Evidence tiers

### Structural gate

Machine evidence for:

- exact candidate identity;
- one authored advance per beat;
- decision before action commitment;
- truthful objective mechanics;
- in-play revelation;
- accepted result;
- persistent world delta;
- record, recovery, resume, and successor;
- no required network dependency.

Structural evidence does not prove production representation.

### Representation release gate

Machine evidence tied to the same candidate:

- exact role-plan and production-coverage bytes;
- original provenance and source confinement;
- complete production coverage with zero prototype roles;
- runtime-mounted production identities across all six surfaces;
- desktop, portrait, and short-landscape screenshots;
- protected-world geometry;
- reduced-motion, forced-colors, and nonvisual equivalents;
- exact represented resume;
- no outbound asset dependency.

A structural pass plus representation failure is an **authored-logic prototype** or **representation rework**, not a machine-qualified pilot.

### Screenshot review

Screenshots are review inputs, not self-validating receipts. Before product classification, they must be inspected for:

- obstruction;
- cropping and staging;
- visual coherence;
- prototype residue;
- legibility of actor, mechanism, consequence, and successor;
- consistency with declared production sources.

Screenshot existence alone proves nothing about quality.

### Blind-player receipt

Observational evidence from an independent player:

- no assistance, source, or walkthrough knowledge;
- elapsed time to first decision and consequence;
- independently stated answers to the five questions;
- hesitation, wrong turns, loss, retry, reload, pause, and abandonment;
- whether the player voluntarily continues after consequence.

A developer, scripted persona, or model given expected answers may produce diagnostics but not the blind-player receipt.

## Refusal examples

The standard refuses a candidate that:

- jumps from optional premise directly into combat;
- teaches controls but not cause;
- stores story in JSON while rendering only kill waves;
- resolves plumbing labels through enemy counts;
- reveals meaning only on the result screen;
- remembers choice only as copy;
- mutates campaign state before authority acceptance;
- names an unimplemented successor;
- borrows dignity from unrelated repository assets;
- declares many role IDs backed by one prototype renderer and calls them many production assets;
- generates screenshots without examining whether UI covers the game;
- keeps touch controls “inside the stage” while they obscure most of the stage;
- renders production art on desktop and generic substitutes on mobile;
- passes bots but has no independent comprehension evidence.

## Classification ladder

```text
action law without accepted continuing consequence
  -> action-receiver prototype

authored causal chain without complete production representation
  -> authored-logic prototype / representation rework

structural PASS
production representation PASS
blind receipt absent
  -> machine-qualified-authored-pilot

same exact candidate
blind receipt PASS
  -> accepted-playable-authored-episode
```

A playable authored episode is the accepted result of the full conjunctive gate, not a synonym for successful deterministic completion.

No stronger language is permitted by green unrelated asset inventories, role-plan counts, successful bots, or unreviewed screenshots.
