## Player profile

A player profile must select one production adapter, map all seventeen Arc cue identities, refuse the diagnostic primitive adapter, bind authored player and enemy prefabs, disable primitive fallbacks, retain zero Unity physics authority, and preserve the selected Arc timing profile in the provisional candidate.

The production presenter forwards semantic cues through generic animation parameters and a structured Unity event. Camera, VFX, audio, haptic, HUD, subtitle, and accessibility systems remain replaceable cue consumers rather than gameplay scripts.

## Evidence boundary

The hosted workflow proves exact Arc and floor identity, timing-profile projection, Arc-to-C# cue parity, candidate timing custody, exact Arc replay, cue coverage, player-profile refusal, complete World regression, and a pristine checkout.

It does not prove Unity 6000 import, scene serialization, Windows-player feel, controller feel, human comprehension, Quest, or physical acceptance.
