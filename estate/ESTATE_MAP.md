# Estate Map

The estate is a coordinated set of authorities, products, publications, and receipts. It is intentionally larger than either repository and intentionally smaller than every possible future extension.

## Source and execution plane

`axm-arc` owns the deterministic engine, registered source planes, canonical reference builders, save migrations, portable-run envelopes, connected-operation custody, and creator-facing source artifacts. The exact local-estate branch includes the deterministic production-build correction required for reproducible reconstruction.

`axm-world` owns the Rodoh player, cartridge shelf, local storage transactions, neutral imported-cartridge presentation, accessibility, localization, first-party production assets, browser qualification, and the local-estate orchestrator. World vendors the Arc paths declared in `src/engine/VENDORED_FROM`; those files remain Arc authority even when physically present in World.

## Product plane

The estate contains five first-party programs and one deliberately unaffiliated clean-room cartridge. The first-party references prove production depth across the Open Universe, dungeon descent, and Common Ship management. The Orchard at Low Tide proves that an unfamiliar generic Arc can enter through the ordinary file path, remain holder-owned, use neutral presentation, complete its campaign, export changed state, and resume without a new receiver exception.

Each current product identity is pinned in `estate.lock.json`. Historical identities remain attributable evidence and never resolve as aliases for revised authored law.

## Publication plane

The publication library contains Books I through IV and the Second Recension Addenda. Books I through III and the Addenda describe the implemented v1.0 canon. Book IV is reviewed publication canon and has staged post-1.0 development contracts, but no Book IV source plane, compiler, receiver, or Program is active in this estate.

The publication ZIP, its manifest, and checksum ledger are copied into the snapshot. They are human-readable context and canon custody, not executable configuration.

## Presentation and asset plane

World holds the constitutional Rodoh mark, cartridge-bay framing, first-party environments, portraits, motifs, cross-sections, symbol atlases, accessibility fallbacks, and procedural sensory roots. The snapshot preserves source SVGs and runtime output through source archives, static builds, documentation, and checksums. Neutral imported cartridges are expressly prevented from inheriting first-party fiction or art.

## Local mutable plane

`.rodoh-estate` is the only mutable local operating area created by the launcher. Its caches can be rebuilt. Its builds can be reproduced. Its logs explain failures. Its receipts establish what passed. Its snapshots preserve a complete recovery path. None of these folders silently modifies Git authority.

## Evidence plane

The automated receipt chain is:


The terminal receipt is `local-operator-acceptance`. It binds the exact automated chain to observed local usability and the preserved snapshot. The final snapshot should contain all of these receipts.

## Future plane

Book IV begins only after the v1.0 estate is locally accepted and released. Its staged Arc and World contracts preserve the Lineage Commons program without adding a hidden dependency to the current release.
